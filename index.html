02 / NAZRA STORY

NAZRA STORY

There are things you do not see when you look at them but only when you look through them We do not always remember faces and we do not always remember the words that were said but we remember one gaze that changed something inside us without knowing how

A gaze is not a moment it is a meaning it is a feeling that passes between two people without a sound and a small detail that may remain in memory longer than it should

This is where NAZRA came from Not to tell you who you are and not to give you a shape to wear but to leave you the space to say something about yourself without speaking

We believe true difference does not need to ask for attention and what truly catches the eye is not what tries to be seen but what makes you look twice

NAZRA Because some things are not understood the first time

FAQ

How can I pay?
You can pay through Vodafone Cash, InstaPay or Cash on Delivery.
